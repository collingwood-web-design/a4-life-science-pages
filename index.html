About us

Marcelo Morales

Life sciences executive, entrepreneur, and innovator

Originally from Chile, degrees from McMaster University (Biochemistry) and University of Toronto (MBA) before working in life sciences management consulting at Deloitte. He relocated from Canada to the USA to become the CEO of Jubilant HollisterStier. Following his tenure there, he founded A4 Ventures, a health technology investment firm.

He founded or co-founded Allele Diagnostics, a clinical genomics company; Celere Talent, a leading biotech talent acquisition firm; Amend Health, a health care advisory firm, among other business ventures. He has been involved in civic leadership, having served as board chair for a chamber of commerce, board president for the Innovia Foundation, board member for Life Sciences Washington, and a board member for the United Way.
