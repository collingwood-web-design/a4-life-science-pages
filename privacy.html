Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

A4 Life Sciences (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with applicable privacy law, including Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies.

Who we are

A4 Life Sciences (A4LifeScience Inc)
Email: info@a4lifesciences.com
Phone: 509-315-6625

Information we collect

We collect personal information that you choose to give us, and limited technical information created when you use the site.

Contact form. If you submit the form on this website, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, organisation, and message, so we can respond to your enquiry.

Email and phone. If you contact us directly using the email address or phone number on this site, we keep the information you share so we can respond.

Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting, content delivery, form delivery, and site monitoring providers may automatically receive standard request data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and timestamps. We do not use this to build advertising profiles about you.

How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries you send us
  • Operate and maintain this website
  • Improve reliability and security of the site
  • Meet legal or professional obligations when required

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share personal information only when needed to run this website or when the law requires it:

  • Website and form service providers. Form submissions are processed through our website service providers so messages can be delivered to us. Site files and images are served through hosting and content delivery network (CDN) providers.
  • Site monitoring. Limited technical event data may be collected to help monitor site performance and availability.
  • Legal requests. We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not set its own advertising or marketing tracking cookies. It may use your browser’s session storage for a small setting that remembers you have already seen the opening animation during your visit. That setting contains no personal information and is cleared when you close the browser tab.

Third-party services we use, such as Google Fonts or our hosting and CDN providers, may set or read cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. You can clear or block cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

Retention

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond, keep business records, and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it when practical.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; please avoid sending highly sensitive information by email or web form.

Your rights

Subject to applicable privacy law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent
  • Delete information we no longer need to retain

To make a request, email info@a4lifesciences.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party websites and profiles, including partner organisations and LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are their own. We encourage you to read their policies before sharing personal information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

Contact us

Questions about privacy or this policy? Email info@a4lifesciences.com or call 509-315-6625.
